## How make.fun works

- **Open**: The bounty is live and open for participants to review tasks, gather context, and begin building.
- **Submitted**: Participants upload their public repositories accompanied by full AI session transcripts prior to the deadline.
- **Checked**: Submissions undergo verification checks to ensure compliance with all contest rules and guidelines.
- **Scored**: Validated entries are evaluated and scored according to implementation quality and requirements.
- **Paid**: USDC prizes are securely released from on-chain escrow straight to the winners' wallets.

### Good to know
- Most bounties are completely free to enter.
- Only one entry per contest is allowed per account.
- Entries that pass the verification checks but do not reach paid ranks can still qualify for participation rewards.

## What you need

- An AI coding agent such as Gemini CLI, Claude Code, or Codex to help build the project.
- A GitHub account for hosting your public code repository.
- Node.js 22 or newer for running your development environment.
- A Solana wallet to claim your USDC rewards.

## 1. Create your account

Start by visiting our referral link at https://make.fun/r/ja4wxs2k and click Sign in. You can authenticate using GitHub, Google, Apple, Discord, or a Solana wallet. Joining is completely free, and signing in with a wallet does not submit any transactions. Once logged in, complete your profile details and link both your GitHub account and Solana wallet.

## 2. Pick a bounty

Browse through the available bounties on the main page, which are organized by Trending, New, or Ending soon across categories like Code, Games, Trading, Forecasting, and Growth. Carefully review the project description, rules, scoring criteria, prize distribution, and deadlines before deciding.

## 3. Get the kit

Download the official bounty kit package for your chosen challenge. From within the kit folder, run the command `node cli/makefun.mjs login --origin https://make.fun` and approve the code prompt to ensure your entries are submitted under your account.

## 4. Build your entry

Build your entry using your AI coding agent during a recorded session. Submit your public repository along with your complete session transcript, as the transcript proves your AI agent actually built it.

## 5. Submit it

Submit your work through the kit using your repository URL, commit SHA, transcript bundle, and title. Finally, cryptographically sign the entry message with your Solana wallet.

## 6. Check it, fix it, resubmit

If any verification checks fail, address the issues and resubmit your updated entry before the contest deadline ends.

## 7. Claim your reward

If your entry secures a paid ranking position, you can claim your USDC prize straight from the escrow to your Solana wallet.
